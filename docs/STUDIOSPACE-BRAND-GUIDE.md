# StudioSpace brand direction

## Brand idea

StudioSpace is a marketplace for the people and places behind creative work. The identity combines an open studio doorway with an aperture: a place to enter, frame a shot and make something.

## Visual system

- Ink: `#191D1A` — structure, navigation and primary text.
- Warm paper: `#F4F1EB` — the main canvas.
- Vermilion: `#DF5A43` — action and emphasis.
- Soft sage: `#DFE6DC` — secondary surfaces and quiet accents.
- Editorial type: Manrope / DM Sans for interface text; Playfair Display for selected expressive headings.
- Photography: real studio, production, workshop and daylight interiors. Avoid generic abstract gradients and artificial decorative effects.

## Logo usage

Use `client/public/studiospace-mark.svg` for the standalone mark. The horizontal lockup is composed in the navbar and footer from the mark plus the lowercase StudioSpace wordmark. Keep the mark on warm paper or a dark ink surface; preserve clear space around it and do not stretch or recolour it.

## Interface principles

1. Lead with practical information: category, location, capacity, equipment, rate and availability.
2. Keep discovery calm and scannable; use filters that answer real booking questions.
3. Treat listing photography as the primary visual asset.
4. Use motion only to explain state or guide focus, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
5. Keep forms explicit, accessible and reassuring, with clear errors and confirmation states.
6. Never imply insurance, independent identity verification or staffed 24/7 support unless the service actually provides it.

## Content voice

Use direct, specific language. Describe what is included and what the visitor should do next. Avoid filler claims, fake popularity metrics, stock phrases and decorative copy.
